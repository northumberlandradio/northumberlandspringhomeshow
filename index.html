    Think of the guide as the local best-of book for home, garden and leisure, like a yellow pages for the businesses people want to hire.

    Free in every visitor's handsThe guide is handed out free at the show, and attendees take it home with them.
    Kept long after the weekendPeople hold on to it and pull it out when the deck, roof or kitchen project finally comes up.
    Found again after the showVisitors who met you at your booth can look you up later, without hunting for a business card.
